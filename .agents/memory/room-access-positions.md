---
name: Room access by position
description: Why account roles alone cannot distinguish excluded cooks from other reporters
---
Room-management exclusions must consider the account's “Zanimanje / Pozicija” as well as its technical role.

**Why:** The user confirmed that cooks are identified by that position field. Housekeepers, floor supervisors and cooks can share the reporter/reception role; excluding or admitting that entire role would contradict the requested access.

**How to apply:** Preserve the position-based cook exclusion when changing room permissions. Keep actual reception accounts scoped to their assigned hotel, rather than treating every reporter as reception.
